Definitions and interpretation

TF = total fixation duration divided by the number of distinct fixated words (milliseconds). SR = 1 − distinct fixated words / text words. RR = backward word transitions / text words. Values describe the selected complete path, not the current playback frame. Empty paths have undefined indicators.

Circle area is proportional to duration; numbers indicate fixation order. Blue connections move forward or stay at the same word; orange connections move backward. Heatmaps sum duration at each word for the selected path and share one color scale across panels.

Both models use the reconstructed PhraseBank-only training route, without subsequent fine-tuning. ScanDL 2.0 uses DDIM-50. FinGaze uses word-level Qwen states in training and global states in inference. Texts were sampled uniformly from 123 eligible reconstructed target texts, excluding the three previously displayed texts. Eligibility requires at least two human paths with two or more retained fixations and two nonempty saved paths for each model. Two paths were then sampled uniformly without replacement within each source. Python random.Random(20260907) was used with text hashes and source indices sorted before sampling. No TF, SR, RR, trajectory distance, or model preference entered selection. These are saved outputs, not live inference. Word positions are reflowed for display, not original screen coordinates. Playback omits saccade times. The page shows unweighted FinGaze paths, not a separate FinGaze-SW generator; SW reweights a candidate pool rather than producing different paths. Only two paths per source are included; no population-level inference or human reliability estimate is implied.

This page contains only the selected texts and de-identified trajectories. All included data are public and extractable. No database, model weights, analytics, or backend services are connected. Reader labels are local to each text, not cross-text identities.
