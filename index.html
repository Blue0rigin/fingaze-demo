Definitions and interpretation

TF = total fixation duration divided by the number of distinct fixated words (milliseconds). SR = 1 − distinct fixated words / text words. RR = backward word transitions / text words. Values describe the selected complete path, not the current playback frame. Empty paths have undefined indicators.

Circle area is proportional to duration; numbers indicate fixation order. Blue connections move forward or stay at the same word; orange connections move backward. Heatmaps sum duration at each word for the selected path and share one color scale across panels.

Both models use the reconstructed PhraseBank-only training route, without subsequent fine-tuning. ScanDL 2.0 uses DDIM-50. FinGaze uses word-level Qwen states in training and global states in inference. The two generated examples are the first nonempty attempts in each saved K=50 pool, not the best-performing paths. The human examples are the first two distinct readers in source order with at least two retained fixations; a single fixation does not establish inattention. These are saved outputs, not live inference. Word positions are reflowed for display, not original screen coordinates. Playback omits saccade times. The page shows unweighted FinGaze paths, not a separate FinGaze-SW generator; SW reweights a candidate pool rather than producing different paths. Only two paths per source are included; no population-level inference or human reliability estimate is implied.

This page contains only the selected texts and de-identified trajectories. All included data are public and extractable. No database, model weights, analytics, or backend services are connected. Reader labels are local to each text, not cross-text identities.
